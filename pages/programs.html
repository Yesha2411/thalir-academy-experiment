Data Entry Operator

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a laptop or computer at home?
+

No. All training is done on academy computers. You only need basic reading and basic school-level English familiarity.

Will I get a job after this course?
+

The program prepares you for entry-level office and data-entry roles. Thalir supports placement guidance and connects strong performers with local employers and panchayat offices.

How is this different from a free computer class?
+

This is a structured, six-month program with clear learning stages, assessments, and NSDC-style certification focused on work-ready skills, not just basics.

Are there competitions or fun activities?
+

Yes! We conduct PowerPoint presentation contests where students design and present on their own topics, building confidence and creativity.

6 Months · NSDC Certified

Tailoring & Garment
Production Training

A six-month, NSDC-aligned program that builds strong hand-stitching and machine-based tailoring skills for women and youth. Learners transition from basic stitching to creating full garments that can be sold or used for home-based businesses.

Who It's For

Women and youth who want to earn from sewing at home
School dropouts and homemakers seeking flexible work
Anyone with interest in stitching but no formal training
Vastrika (Malar Society): Market linkages enabling learners to sell garments in exhibitions and access bulk orders. Training → Income → Sustainability.
Enroll Now Talk to a Counsellor
Tailoring Basics

Hand stitching, seams, hems, measuring tape, cutting and stitching simple items

Machine-Based Tailoring

Sewing machine basics, stitching churidars, blouses, salwar kameez, seam finishing

Garment Construction & Finishing

Patterns, professional fitting, basic alterations and repairs

Advanced Tailoring & Production

Advanced blouse designs, precision cutting, bulk stitching, quality control and real customer timelines

Value-Added Skills

Simple Aari embroidery on collars and hems, finishing and packaging for sale

Certification

Theory and practical assessment on stitching and machine handling. Certificate from Thalir Academy.

Do I need a sewing machine at home?
+

No. All training is done on academy machines. Sewing machines can later be provided at subsidized cost with EMI after income generation.

Can I start earning at home after this course?
+

Yes. Many learners start home-based stitching and embroidery work. Strong performers get linked to local customers and exhibitions via Vastrika.

Is this course only for women?
+

No. Both women and men are welcome. It suits anyone interested in tailoring as a livelihood.
