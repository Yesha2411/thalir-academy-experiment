Updates

What's Happening at Thalir Academy

Stay updated with how learning is shaping lives, building confidence, and creating opportunities across communities.

Parenting
Understanding Girlhood: Parenting Awareness Programme

Conducted for 22 parents of girl children — covering physical, emotional, and social development across different life stages. Led by Director Raviraaj Munusamy, emphasising balanced parenting, self-care for mothers, and building a supportive home environment.

Training
Crafting Confidence: From Loom to Laptop

A unique program combining traditional weaving with digital presentation skills. Students created woven belts guided by skilled artisans, then learned to present their handmade creations using computer tools — integrating traditional knowledge with modern technology.

Initiative
Kanini Kani: Early Digital Learning for Young Minds

A foundational digital learning initiative for young students — building curiosity, comfort, and confidence with technology. Sessions introduce computer basics, mouse and keyboard use, Paint, and Notepad through interactive activities. Focuses on building attention, patience, and logical thinking alongside technical skills.

Media
Ooraru Oli — A New Phase of Growth

Five outstanding students selected to form the Ooraru Oli Entrepreneur Team — moving beyond classroom learning into digital media, communication, and entrepreneurship. The team creates promotional videos and success stories for their YouTube channel now with 500+ subscribers. Students gain hands-on experience in editing, design, and content creation.

Community
Wheels of Change — Driving Initiative

Empowering women with driving skills and independence. Staff members supported in learning both two-wheeler and four-wheeler driving. Participants: Two Wheeler — 8, Four Wheeler — 6, Both — 2. "We are not just learning to drive but learning to lead, dream, and become independent."

Holiday Programme
Kalanjiyam Winter Holiday Weaving Initiative

Students from classes 8–12 explored weaving as a living cultural tradition during winter break — developing focus, patience, and creativity through hands-on practice. Students expressed that weaving helped them feel calm, focused, and more attentive. "Weaving skills today, creating possibilities for tomorrow."

Key Initiatives

Programmes That Go Beyond Skills

Thalir Academy's impact extends beyond technical training into holistic development, leadership, and community change.

Suresh — Positive Thinking Sessions

Motivational sessions focusing on positive thinking and self-confidence. Through inspiring stories, real-life examples, and practical exercises including personal affirmations and group discussions, learners understand how to replace negative thoughts and build emotional strength. Teaches that every setback carries a lesson and every difficulty can become a turning point.

Deepa — Overcoming Failure & Resilience

Sessions designed to help learners view setbacks as essential steps toward success — not as the end. Includes inspiring stories of individuals who achieved success through determination and perseverance. Practical exercises help learners analyse failures, identify lessons, and reset goals. Transforms fear of failure into motivation for continuous learning and improvement.

Yokesh Srinivasan — Hardware Program

Hands-on training in computer hardware — motherboard, CPU, RAM, hard disk, power supply, graphic cards. Covers assembling and disassembling computers, troubleshooting boot errors, overheating, hardware conflicts. Practical labs and real-world simulations prepare learners for entry-level IT roles and further certifications.

Kalanjiyam Welcomes Young Changemakers

Hosted six M.A. Development Administration students from Gandhigram University for a one-month immersive internship — working across 18 schools, leading awareness sessions in 3 villages on hygiene, TB, and breast cancer prevention, and conducting mental health surveys at Thalir Academy creating safe spaces for student discussions.

200+ community members engaged
