Support Us

Support Skills. Strengthen Livelihoods. Create Lasting Change.

Your support helps rural youth and women access skill training, build confidence, and move toward employment, self-employment, and long-term independence.

The Need

Why Your Support Matters

Across rural communities, young people and women face limited access to quality education, digital exposure, and livelihood opportunities. Financial constraints, social restrictions, and lack of guidance often prevent them from reaching their potential.

What Your Support Enables

Provide access to skill-based training programmes
Support first-generation learners in digital education
Enable women to build independent livelihoods
Offer counselling and life skills support
Create safe and structured learning environments
Where It Goes

Where Your Support Goes

Scholarships

Supporting learners unable to afford training fees — ensuring equal access to opportunities.

Learning Materials

Notebooks, digital tools, training kits, and course materials for every learner.

Infrastructure

Improving classrooms, training equipment, and learning spaces.

Programs & Counselling

Sustaining digital skills, tailoring, life skills, counselling, and community outreach.
