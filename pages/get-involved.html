Build Livelihoods. Hire Skilled Talent. Partner for Impact.

Thalir Academy connects employers, CSR partners, and institutions with trained rural youth and women ready for employment and livelihood opportunities.

Hire From Us

Access Job-Ready Talent
from Rural Communities

Thalir learners are trained through structured, livelihood-focused programs combining technical learning, communication, discipline, and practical exposure. Our candidates are ready for entry-level roles with confidence and commitment.

Available Roles

Digital & Office Roles
Data entry operators, computer assistants, office support staff
Vocational Roles
Tailors, garment workers, Aari embroidery specialists, beauticians
Emerging Skills
Basic digital design (Canva), communication, community liaison roles

Why Hire From Thalir?

Pre-trained, job-ready candidates
Reduced hiring and training cost
High retention from local hiring
Social impact and business value combined
